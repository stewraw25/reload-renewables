Privacy

How Reload Renewables Ltd handles personal information collected through this website.

Who we are

Reload Renewables Ltd, trading as Reload Renewables & AC, is the controller. Our address is Unit 4, Wrexham Industrial Estate, Wrexham, LL13 9XS. Email hello@reloadrenewables.com or call (01978) 809 500.

What we collect and why

When you ask for a quote or a survey, you use the OpenSolar quotation form on our contact page. You may enter your name, contact details, and property or energy information. We use that information to reply and to prepare a survey or a quote — the steps you ask us to take before a contract. We do not run a separate form on this website.

How long we keep enquiries

We keep enquiry records for 1 year. We keep them for longer only where the law requires it.

Processors

OpenSolar (opensolar.com) provides the quotation form. OpenSolar processes those enquiries for us and sets the cookies that form needs in order to work.

Cookies

Cookies are used so the OpenSolar quotation form can work. We do not currently use Google Analytics or advertising cookies. The cookie notice on each page links to this section.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it. You can ask us to restrict how we use it, and you can object. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. To use these rights, email hello@reloadrenewables.com or call (01978) 809 500.

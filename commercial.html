Commercial solar and battery FAQs

Do you install commercial solar in Wrexham and Chester?
Yes. Units, offices, farms and industrial roofs. Neighbouring units on Wrexham Industrial Estate are the first mile from Unit 4. We do not name them here.
Can a business have battery storage without solar?
Yes — for peak shaving and backup, or with existing solar. Priced on survey in kWp / kWh, not the domestic Silver/Gold/Platinum cards.
What is AIA?
Ask your accountant. We provide a spec pack (kWp, equipment, programme). We do not give tax advice.

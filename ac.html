Air conditioning questions

What about refrigerant rules for air conditioning in Wrexham or Chester?

Refrigerant work is regulated. Ask us on the quote how that part of the job is covered.

Can air conditioning also provide heating?

Yes. The Bosch, Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu and Panasonic systems we fit can also heat a room, including an extension, a home office or a commercial space.

What is the difference between domestic and commercial AC?

Domestic is usually wall-mounted single or multi-split for bedrooms, living rooms and small offices. Commercial often uses cassette units in suspended ceilings, larger outdoor condensers and 3-phase systems for shops, clinics, gyms and offices around Chester and Wrexham.

How often should air conditioning be serviced?

We recommend annual servicing for all systems. Domestic: keeps efficiency high and air quality good. Commercial: regular servicing helps with the warranty and energy efficiency.

Do you install air conditioning in new builds and extensions in North Wales?

Yes. We work closely with builders and homeowners across Wrexham, Chester, Mold and Oswestry on new builds, loft conversions, garden offices and extensions. We can design multi-zone systems from the plans stage.

Are air conditioning prices published as from-prices?

No. Air conditioning is priced after a survey. A guide price can start from a short room video. It is not one of the solar package from-prices.

Can I have battery storage without solar panels?

Do you install batteries for homes and businesses?
Yes. Domestic battery storage is on the homes page. Commercial batteries for units, offices, farms and industrial sites — including Wrexham Industrial Estate — are on the commercial page.
Which batteries do you specify in Wrexham and Chester?
Pylontech on Silver, BYD on Gold and Tesla Powerwall 3 on Platinum, subject to survey. We size the battery to the property and tariff rather than selling one model to everyone.

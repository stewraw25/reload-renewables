How to Choose Solar Installers in Wrexham & Chester

If you're searching for solar installers Wrexham, solar installers Chester or solar PV near you, here are the key things that separate good local companies from the rest.

MCS Certification
Non-negotiable for any serious solar or heat pump work. MCS is required for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS), Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) payments and to protect your consumer rights. Always ask to see current MCS certificates for the company and the specific products.
NICEIC & F-Gas
NICEIC registration proves electrical competence. F-Gas certification is legally required for air conditioning and heat pump work involving refrigerants. Our engineers hold both and we are happy to show ID on site.
Genuine Local Presence
A Wrexham or Chester address (not a virtual office) means faster response for surveys, installs and especially servicing. We are based at Unit 4, Wrexham Industrial Estate LL13 9XS with real projects across Flintshire, Denbighshire and Cheshire West.
Transparent Quotes & Warranties
A proper quote lists exact panel (e.g. DAS 450W), inverter, battery model, scaffolding, DNO fees and the 10-year workmanship warranty. Beware of "from £X" quotes that change later. We provide fixed written quotes after a free site survey.
Realistic Performance Data
Good solar installers model your actual roof orientation, shading and local irradiance (we use ~980 kWh/kWp for Wrexham/Chester area). We also factor your real tariff (Octopus Go, Flux etc) in the savings calculator so you know what to expect.
Aftercare & Servicing
The install is only the start. Annual servicing protects warranties (especially important for F-Gas AC and heat pumps) and keeps performance high. We offer clear maintenance plans and are still here years later for our customers in Wrexham and Chester.

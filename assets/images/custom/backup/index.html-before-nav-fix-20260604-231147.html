COMMON QUESTIONS

Solar Installers Wrexham & Chester FAQs — Common Questions About Solar PV, Batteries, Heat Pumps & AC

Answers to the questions we hear most from customers across Wrexham, Chester, Mold and the surrounding areas.

Do you install solar panels in Wrexham and Chester?⌄

Yes — we are local MCS accredited solar panel installers covering Wrexham, Chester, Mold, Oswestry, Flintshire and all of North Wales. We handle full design, DNO applications, scaffolding and MCS registration.

What grants are available for heat pumps in North Wales?⌄

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) provides grants of up to £7,500 towards air source heat pumps. We are experienced in grant-ready designs and can handle the full application for properties in Wrexham, Chester and surrounding areas.

Can I get 0% finance for solar or battery storage?⌄

Yes. 0% interest finance options are available through our authorised broker partners, ensuring full compliance with UK consumer credit regulations. Ask us about interest-free plans for solar PV, batteries or heat pumps.

Do you install battery storage without solar panels?⌄

Absolutely. Many customers in Wrexham and Chester install battery-only systems on tariffs like Octopus Go or Intelligent Octopus Go. Charge cheaply overnight and discharge during the day for big bill savings.

How long does a typical solar + battery installation take?⌄

A standard domestic solar + battery system usually takes 1–3 days. We manage everything including permits, DNO and commissioning. Larger commercial jobs (e.g. 58kWp) take longer and we provide full project plans.

Are you F-Gas certified for air conditioning?⌄

Yes. All our engineers are F-Gas certified for the installation, servicing and repair of air conditioning systems (Mitsubishi, Daikin, LG and more) across Wrexham, Chester and North Wales.

What warranties do you offer on solar and batteries?⌄

10-year workmanship warranty on all installations. Panels typically 25–30 years, BYD batteries 10–12 years, Tesla Powerwall 10 years. Annual servicing helps protect your warranties.

Do you service and maintain existing solar or AC systems?⌄

Yes — we offer annual servicing and maintenance plans for solar PV, batteries, heat pumps and air conditioning. Regular servicing keeps systems efficient, protects warranties and is essential for F-Gas compliance on AC.

Who are the best solar installers in Wrexham and Chester?⌄

Reload Renewables & AC are local MCS and NICEIC accredited solar installers based in Wrexham at Unit 4, Wrexham Industrial Estate. We deliver premium solar PV (DAS 450W), battery storage and heat pumps for homes and businesses across Wrexham, Chester, Mold, Oswestry and North Wales, with transparent pricing and a 10-year workmanship guarantee.

How do I choose the right solar installers in Wrexham or Chester?⌄

Prioritise MCS certification (required for grants, SEG payments and warranties), NICEIC electrical and F-Gas accreditations, genuine local presence with recent projects in your area, written quotes with clear equipment specs, and independent reviews. We provide free no-obligation site surveys and only recommend systems that suit your property and budget.

Our mission

Reload Renewables was set up after years of seeing badly fitted, unsafe solar systems, and after our own clients asked us to do the job properly. We fit quality products to a high installation standard, with a calm, friendly service from the first visit to switch-on.

That means a proper look at the property, a written quote, and time to decide. There is no pressure to sign on the day. The same Wrexham team also fits battery storage and air conditioning, and stays on for servicing after switch-on. Heat pumps are coming soon.

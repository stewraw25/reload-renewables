Our Mission

To power homes and businesses across Wrexham, Chester and North Wales with reliable, high-quality renewable energy and climate control solutions — delivered by local engineers who care about doing the job right.

At Reload Renewables & AC our mission is simple: make clean, efficient energy accessible and dependable for local people and businesses. We install premium solar PV, battery storage, heat pumps and air conditioning with the highest standards of workmanship, transparency and aftercare.

We are a genuinely local team based in Wrexham, serving Chester, Mold, Oswestry, Flintshire, Denbighshire and the wider North Wales & North West area. Every project — whether a 3-bed home or a commercial site — gets the same attention to detail, proper design, and long-term support.

What drives us

  • Quality first — We only use Tier-1 equipment (DAS, Enphase, Tesla, BYD, Daikin, Mitsubishi, LG) with proper warranties and MCS/NICEIC/F-Gas compliance.
  • Honest advice — We size systems realistically for your roof, usage and tariffs (we use ~980 kWh/kWp for this area) and never oversell.
  • Local & accountable — Real address, real engineers, real aftercare. We are still here for servicing years after the install.
  • Future focused — Helping homes and businesses cut energy costs, reduce carbon and gain energy independence with solar + storage and heat pumps.

“Future Looks Bright...” isn’t just our strapline — it’s what we deliver every day for our customers in Wrexham and Chester.

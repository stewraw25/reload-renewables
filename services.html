If you are comparing solar installers in Wrexham or Chester, these are the standards we hold ourselves to: careful fitting, a written quote, and time to decide.

NICEIC & F-Gas
NICEIC registration proves electrical competence. F-Gas certification is legally required for air conditioning and heat pump work involving refrigerants. Our engineers hold both and we are happy to show ID on site.
Genuine Local Presence
We are based at Unit 4, Wrexham Industrial Estate, LL13 9XS, and we cover Flintshire, Denbighshire and Cheshire West for surveys, installs and servicing.
Transparent Quotes & Warranties
A proper quote lists the panel (for example DAS 450W), inverter, battery, scaffolding, DNO and the 10-year workmanship warranty. We write that quote after a free survey, and there is no pressure to sign on the day.
Realistic Performance Data
Good solar installers model your actual roof orientation, shading and local irradiance (we use ~980 kWh/kWp for Wrexham/Chester area). We also factor your real tariff (Octopus Go, Flux etc) in the savings calculator so you know what to expect.
Aftercare & Servicing
Switch-on is not the end. Annual servicing protects warranties, especially on F-Gas air conditioning and heat pumps. We offer maintenance plans and stay available afterwards in Wrexham and Chester.

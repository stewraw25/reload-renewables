Home solar and battery FAQs

Do you install solar and batteries on houses in Wrexham and Chester?
Yes. This page is for homes. We also cover Mold, Oswestry, Flintshire, Deeside, Flint and Denbighshire. Commercial roofs are on the commercial page.
Can I have a home battery without solar?
Yes. A house can have a battery on an off-peak tariff. You can add solar later. See the battery storage page.
How much do home solar packages start from?
Silver from £8,000 and Platinum from £14,000, subject to survey. Yields are modelled at about 980 kWh/kWp for this area. Detail is on the solar installation page.

No. Units, offices, farms and industrial are on the commercial solar and battery page.

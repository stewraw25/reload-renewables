What do MCS and DNO mean for my solar installation?⌄

MCS is the certification scheme used for most UK solar grants, warranties and export payments. DNO is the grid application (Scottish Power in this area). We include both in a typical solar quote.

CERTIFIED & TRUSTED BY

What each mark is for. Certificates on request. We do not publish an MCS number on this site.

MCS

Microgeneration Certification Scheme. Needed for most solar warranties, grants and Smart Export Guarantee. Find an installer.

NICEIC

Electrical competence for the installation — wiring, isolation and testing to UK standards.

RECC
RECC

Renewable Energy Consumer Code — deposit protection and a complaints route for home energy sales. Member directory.

TrustMark

Government-endorsed quality mark for trades that meet a recognised standard.

HIES

Home energy consumer code covering deposit protection and workmanship insurance on qualifying systems.

F-Gas is held for air-conditioning and heat-pump refrigerant work. Tesla Powerwall 3 is a product we specify on Platinum, not an installer award listed here.

HOW A JOB RUNS

Survey to handover — five steps

Start to finish with the same team. We take it at your pace, and there is no pressure to sign on the day.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Use the quote chooser for solar, battery, air conditioning, a future heat pump, or servicing.

  2. 2

    Remote look, then a survey

    We look at what you send, then survey the property. There is no obligation to go ahead.

  3. 3

    Written quote with warranty in writing

    A written quote after the survey, with the 6-year labour and most-equipment warranty in writing. Take time to decide.

  4. 4

    Grid connection, where it applies

    G98 or G99 where the connection needs it. Every qualifying installation is signed off and MCS certified through an MCS-certified installer partner, named on your quote. Reload's own MCS certification is awaiting approval.

  5. 5

    Install and handover

    Walkthrough, app setup, a snag process, and RELOAD Gold Care aftercare.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Solar installation and battery storage FAQs

Questions people ask before booking a survey in Wrexham, Chester and the surrounding areas.

Do you install solar in Wrexham and Chester?⌄

Yes. We install solar on homes and businesses in Wrexham and Chester, including Mold, Oswestry, Flintshire, Deeside, Flint and Denbighshire. Design and DNO are included. See solar installation.

Do you cover Mold, Oswestry, Deeside and Flint?⌄

Yes. Surrounding areas include Mold, Oswestry, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Deeside, Flint and nearby North Wales towns. We are based at Jubilee Manor, Sun Lane, Wrexham, LL13 9RW.

Do you install battery storage without solar?⌄

Yes. We fit batteries with solar or on their own for off-peak tariffs, for homes and commercial sites. Whole-home backup is the Platinum package. See battery storage.

Do you do commercial solar and battery as well as homes?⌄

Yes. Homes are on the domestic page. Units, offices, farms and industrial roofs are on the commercial page. We do not list named clients here.

What about the grid application (DNO) for my solar installation?⌄

DNO is the grid application (Scottish Power in this area).

How much do solar packages start from?⌄

Silver starts from £8,000, Gold from £10,000 and Platinum from £14,000. These are from-prices, subject to survey. 0% VAT on most domestic installations until 31 March 2027. Yields are modelled at about 980 kWh/kWp for this area. Packages are on the solar page.

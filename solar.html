Solar installation FAQs — Wrexham and Chester

Do you install solar in Wrexham and Chester on homes and businesses?
Yes. Domestic solar is for houses in Wrexham, Chester, Mold, Oswestry, Deeside and Flint. Commercial solar is for units, offices, farms and industrial roofs, including Wrexham Industrial Estate.
How much do solar packages start from?
Silver starts from £8,000 and Platinum from £14,000 for typical domestic systems, subject to survey. Prices include design, installation and DNO application. 0% VAT applies for most domestic properties.
What about the grid application (DNO)?
DNO is the grid application — Scottish Power in this area.
Will solar work in North Wales weather?
Yes. Quotes use a modelled yield of about 980 kWh/kWp for this area rather than a sunnier region’s figures. Overcast days still generate.
Can I add battery storage later?
Yes. Systems are specified battery-ready so you can add Pylontech, BYD or Tesla Powerwall 3 later. You can also start with battery only — see the battery storage page.
Do I need planning permission for solar in Wrexham or Chester?
Most domestic roof-mounted solar in Wrexham, Chester, Mold and surrounding areas is permitted development. Listed buildings and some commercial or ground-mount systems may need planning; we check that on survey.
